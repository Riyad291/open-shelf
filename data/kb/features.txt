# Searching the library

The library page searches titles, authors and descriptions. On the full site
it matches whole words and their forms, so a search for "programs" also finds
"programming", and results can be sorted by best match, title, newest or most
opened. Topic buttons narrow the list to one subject or language.

# Searching by picture

On the search page you can upload a photograph of a book cover, and the site
finds the matching book by comparing the picture with the covers and
illustrations it knows.

# Shelf Scan

Shelf Scan takes a photograph of a whole bookshelf, finds the individual
books in it, names the ones it recognises, and recommends more in the same
vein.

# The assistant

The assistant answers questions about this site from these help files, and
says where each answer came from. Some questions depend on who is asking:
members can ask about their own reviews and standing, trusted contributors
about the recommendation lists, and the administrator about catalogue
statistics, accounts and moderation.

Questions the help files do not cover can be passed to a language model
running on the same machine through Ollama, and the answer is labelled as
coming from the model. If no model is running, the assistant says so rather
than guessing.

# Privacy

Open Shelf stores your username, a salted and hashed password, the reviews you
write, the books you look at, and any face photographs you enrol. Photographs
are stored on the server only for signing in and are never shared.

Passwords are stored with PBKDF2, so the password itself is never kept. Your
questions to the assistant are answered on the same server; nothing is sent
to an outside AI service.
