# Accounts and roles

Anyone can browse, search and open every book without an account. Visitors
can also rate a book and leave a comment.

There are four kinds of user. A visitor is anyone not signed in. A member is
anyone who has created an account. A trusted contributor is a member whose
reviews have earned extra weight. The administrator manages the catalogue,
the accounts and moderation.

Creating an account is free and needs only a username and a password of at
least four characters. No email address is required.

# Becoming a trusted contributor

A member becomes a trusted contributor automatically after writing three
reviews that pass moderation. Blocked reviews do not count towards the three.

A trusted contributor's star rating counts double in a book's average, and
they can see the recommended and not-recommended lists in the assistant.

Trusted status can be lost. A trusted contributor who collects more than two
moderation warnings goes back to being a member and is not promoted again
automatically.

# Signing in with your face

Members can sign in by looking at their camera instead of typing a password.
To set it up, open your account page and enrol five photographs from your
browser's camera. After that, choose "Sign in with your face" on the sign-in
screen. The recogniser is trained only on the photographs you enrol.

Your password always keeps working, so face sign-in is optional. You can
remove your photographs from your account page at any time.

# Suspension and reopening

A member who collects more than two moderation warnings is suspended. A
suspended account cannot sign in or post reviews. Only the administrator can
reopen a suspended account, and may choose whether to clear its warnings.
