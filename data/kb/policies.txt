# Returns and refunds

Unopened new books may be returned within 30 days of purchase with proof of
purchase for a full refund to the account balance. Second-hand and clearance
titles are sold as seen and cannot be returned unless they are misdescribed.

A refund is credited to the customer's store account balance, not to a card. A
customer who wants cash back should ask the super-user at the counter.

Damaged or misprinted copies are replaced free of charge at any time, with no
time limit, even without a receipt, provided the title is still in print.

# Account balance

Every registered and VIP customer keeps a prepaid balance on their account.
Purchases are taken from that balance. Money can be added at the counter or by
asking the super-user to credit the account.

If a customer tries to buy a book that costs more than their balance, the
purchase is refused and a one-dollar administration fine is charged to the
account. This fine exists to discourage repeated failed checkouts, which cost
staff time.

Any account whose balance falls below zero is suspended immediately. A suspended
account cannot log in or buy anything. To reopen it, the customer must ask the
super-user to deposit enough money to bring the balance back above zero.

# VIP membership

A registered customer who makes more than three purchases, or who spends more
than two hundred dollars in total, is promoted to VIP automatically. VIP
customers receive ten per cent off every purchase from that point onward and get
access to the shop's to-order and no-order lists.

VIP status is not time-limited and does not need renewal. It can, however, be
lost: a VIP customer who collects more than two moderation warnings is demoted
back to registered customer.

# Reviews and community guidelines

Any logged-in customer may rate a book from one to five stars and leave a
comment. Reviews from customers who actually bought the book carry double weight
in the displayed average, because a verified reader's opinion is worth more.

We keep a list of taboo words. Any such word in a comment is replaced with
asterisks. A comment containing more than three taboo words is blocked entirely
and its author receives a warning. A registered customer who collects more than
two warnings has their account suspended; a VIP customer is demoted instead.

Warnings are cleared when the super-user reinstates an account.

# Ordering decisions

A title that collects more than three ratings with a weighted average below two
stars goes on the no-order list, and we stop reordering it. A title with more
than three ratings and a weighted average above four stars goes on the to-order
list and we keep it permanently in stock.

# Privacy

We store a customer's username, account balance, purchase history, browsing
history, and any face or voice samples they enrol for login. Biometric samples
never leave the shop's own computer and are not shared with anyone. A customer
may ask the super-user to delete their samples or their whole account at any
time.
