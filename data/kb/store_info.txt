# About Inkwell & Co.

Inkwell & Co. is an independent bookshop at 214 Mercer Street, Riverside, with a
second reading room on the first floor. The shop opened in 2009 as a single
second-hand stall and now carries roughly four thousand titles across
literature, science, history, philosophy, poetry, children's books, technology
and biography.

We specialise in public-domain classics and reprints of out-of-print science
writing. If a title is out of print we will search our partner network and
usually find a copy within two weeks at no extra charge.

# Opening hours

Saturday: 10:00 to 18:00
Sunday: 12:00 to 17:00

The first-floor reading room closes one hour before the shop.

# Getting here

We are a four-minute walk from Riverside Central Station, exit 2. The number 14
and number 31 buses stop directly outside. There is metered street parking on
Mercer Street and a paid car park on Alder Lane, two blocks north. The ground
floor is step-free; the reading room is reachable by lift.

# Events

We host a free reading group on the last Thursday of every month at 18:30 in the
first-floor reading room. No booking is needed and the first drink is on us.
Author signings are announced on the noticeboard by the till and in this
system's chat box.
