# The team

Inkwell & Co. is run by four people and one shop cat.

Margaret Osei - owner and buyer
Margaret founded the shop in 2009 after fifteen years as an academic librarian.
She does all the buying and decides what goes on the to-order list. She reads
mostly history and is the person to ask about tracking down an out-of-print
title. In the system she holds the super-user account.

Daniel Reyes - floor manager
Daniel handles the counter, the reading-group evenings and the second-hand
intake. He has an unreasonable enthusiasm for science fiction and will talk
about it for as long as you let him. He is in the shop Tuesday to Saturday.

Priya Raman - cataloguer
Priya maintains the catalogue, writes the shelf descriptions and photographs the
covers used by this system's picture search. She is a poetry specialist and
curates the poetry table by the window.

Tomas Lindqvist - evenings and weekends
Tomas covers evening and Sunday shifts while finishing a philosophy degree. He
runs the children's story hour on Sunday afternoons.

Mielikki - shop cat
Adopted 2017. Sleeps on the philosophy shelf. Does not assist customers.

# Who to ask

For a book you cannot find, ask Daniel at the counter.
For a special order or an out-of-print search, ask Margaret.
For a mistake in a catalogue entry or a missing cover image, ask Priya.
For anything about your account, balance, suspension or warnings, ask Margaret,
who is the only person with super-user access.

# This system

The smart bookstore system was built as a software engineering project. It
handles four kinds of user, logs customers in by face or voice, searches the
catalogue by keyword or by photograph, moderates reviews against the taboo list,
and answers questions in this chat box. Questions about the shop are answered
from these files; anything else is passed to a local language model running on
the shop's own computer, so no customer question is ever sent to an outside
service.
