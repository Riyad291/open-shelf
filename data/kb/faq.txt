# Frequently asked questions

Do you buy second-hand books?
Yes. Bring them to the counter any day except Sunday. Daniel will look through
them and offer either cash or store credit, and credit is always worth about
twenty per cent more than the cash offer. We cannot take textbooks older than
four editions, ex-library copies, or anything water-damaged.

Can I reserve a book?
Yes, for seven days. Ask at the counter or buy it through this system and
collect it later.

Do you ship?
We post within the country by tracked mail. Shipping is a flat four dollars and
is free on orders above fifty dollars. Orders placed before 14:00 go out the
same working day. We do not ship outside the country.

Do you have gift cards?
Yes, in any amount from ten dollars upward. They never expire. A gift card can
be loaded straight onto a customer's account balance.

Is there a cafe?
No, but the reading room has a water fountain and we do not mind covered drinks
from the cafe two doors down. Please keep them off the poetry table.

Can I use the reading room without buying anything?
Yes, always. It has twelve seats, free wifi, and power at six of the desks. The
wifi password is on the card by the door.

How do I log in with my face?
Ask the super-user to enrol you. They will take five photographs at the counter,
which takes about a minute, and train the recogniser. After that the login
screen will identify you from the camera. You can also enrol a voice sample
instead, or both. If the camera does not recognise you, your password always
works.

Why was my comment blocked?
Comments with more than three words from our taboo list are blocked
automatically and earn a warning. If you think this was a mistake, ask Margaret
to review it; she can see the original text and clear the warning.

Why can the assistant not answer my question?
Some information is limited by account type. Visitors see public catalogue
information. Registered customers also see their own account and personalised
recommendations. VIP customers additionally see the shop's order lists. Only the
super-user can see finances, the full account list and moderation records.

Do you take card payments?
At the counter, yes. Inside this system all purchases come out of the prepaid
account balance.

What happens to my data if I close my account?
Everything is deleted, including any face or voice samples, within one working
day. Ask Margaret.
