# Ratings and reviews

Any book can be rated from one to five stars, with an optional comment.
Visitors may rate books too; a signed-in member's rating is kept with their
account, and each member can review a book once. Members can edit their own
review later.

A book's displayed average is weighted: a trusted contributor's rating counts
twice, everyone else's once.

# Moderation and taboo words

Comments are checked against a list of taboo words that the administrator
maintains. A taboo word in a comment is replaced with asterisks and the rest
of the comment is posted.

A comment containing more than three taboo words is blocked entirely, and its
author receives a warning. Editing a review is checked the same way as
posting one, so a blocked edit also earns a warning. The original text of a
blocked comment is kept so the administrator can review it.

More than two warnings suspends a member, or returns a trusted contributor to
member.

# Recommended and not-recommended lists

A book with more than three ratings and a weighted average above four stars
goes on the recommended list. A book with more than three ratings and a
weighted average below two stars goes on the not-recommended list, and is
never suggested to anyone.

# Personal recommendations

The shelf page shows three picks. A visitor sees the books readers here have
reviewed most. A signed-in member sees picks based on the subjects and
authors of the books they have browsed and reviewed, with a sentence saying
why each was chosen.
